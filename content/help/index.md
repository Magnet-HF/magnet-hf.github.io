## Find your next step

- [Browse the software directory](/software/) for official manuals and download pages.
- [Read operational guidance](/operational-guidance/) for station integration.
- [Contact MagNet](https://magnethf.com/contact-us/) for network-specific questions.
- [Report a library issue](https://github.com/Magnet-HF/magnet-hf.github.io/issues/new/choose) for broken links, corrections, or a requested guide.

When reporting a software problem, include the radio model, operating system, application versions, interface, expected behavior, and actual result. Remove personal information and credentials from logs before sharing.
