## Installation & verification

Use the [flmsg manual](https://w1hkj.org/flmsg-help/) for the custom-forms folder location on your platform. Do not assume a Linux path applies on Windows or macOS.

Before trusting a public key, confirm its fingerprint through an established MagNet contact. This library does not yet record a verified fingerprint or key-import guide.

New configuration examples and public templates can be added through the [contribution workflow](/contribute/). Files remain at one maintained location, with links from related pages.
