## Technical guidance

**Currently suggested version: awaiting MagNet confirmation.** The publisher lists **4.0.24** as of October 23, 2026. This is a reference version, not a recorded MagNet station test.

Use the official manual to locate custom forms on your platform. [MagNet forms](/resources/) remain available from the existing website. The MagNet SitRep form is for reports to MagNet S2. [fldigi](/software/fldigi/) and [flamp](/software/flamp/) provide related digital traffic workflows.
