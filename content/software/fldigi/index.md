## Technical guidance

**Currently suggested version: awaiting MagNet confirmation.** The publisher lists **4.2.13** as of October 23, 2026. This is a reference version, not a recorded MagNet station test.

Choose the appropriate build on the official site. Confirm audio devices and radio-control settings before sending traffic. For structured messages and file workflows, see [flmsg](/software/flmsg/) and [flamp](/software/flamp/).
