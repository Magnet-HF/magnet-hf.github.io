Download links lead to maintained project pages so you can choose the appropriate build. Each tool's **Technical guidance** section identifies a suggested version when one has been established, and separates it from versions merely listed by the publisher.

### Tools that work together

**Messaging:** JS8Call Subspace, SpotterX, CommStat, and VarAC. **Digital traffic:** fldigi, flmsg, and flamp. **Radio control:** flrig. **Local mesh:** Meshtastic. **Planning:** CommsPlan.
