## Technical guidance

**Currently suggested version: awaiting MagNet confirmation.** The publisher lists **2.0.12** as of October 23, 2026. This is a reference version, not a recorded MagNet station test.

Confirm flrig controls your radio before adding another application. Use the model-specific guidance in the official manual. The [Linux Mint bridge guide](/operational-guidance/js8call-rigctld-flrig/) covers an optional JS8Call integration through rigctld.
