## Technical guidance

**Currently suggested version: 4.1.0.502.** Basis: the MagNet build publisher identifies the releases on its page as approved for MagNet stations. Link checked October 23, 2026; no independent station test has been recorded in this library.

The inspected release offers Windows x64 and Debian amd64 packages. Other hardware or platforms require confirmation from the project; do not substitute another JS8Call edition without checking compatibility with your workflow.

Configure the radio's CAT connection and the audio devices, then verify normal operation before connecting companion tools. For companion interfaces, use the application's documented network/API settings and match the connection settings in the companion application.
